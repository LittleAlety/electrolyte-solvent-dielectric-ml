**To:** The Editors, *Physical Chemistry Chemical Physics* (PCCP)

**Re:** Submission of a research article, "Descriptor decision stability in electrolyte
solvent screening: level artefacts, axis-locked disagreement and screening usability in an
auditable five-channel funnel"

Dear Editors,

We submit the enclosed manuscript for consideration at *PCCP*. The work is a decision-centred
study of descriptor stability in electrolyte solvent screening, and it is released with a
complete pre-registration-and-audit record.

**What is new.** (1) We treat the *stability of the decision* induced by a descriptor as the
primary object, not the descriptor’s correlation with a target: a single-variable ladder
separates level artefacts from genuine chemistry. (2) Every reading is pre-registered before it
is computed, and every failed criterion is registered rather than dropped; the main scoreboard
is capped at a published budget (19 attempts, 0 taken this cycle). (3) We report honest negative
results, including an undecidable reduction-axis criterion whose power floor is reported
alongside the null.

**Why *PCCP*.** The paper sits at the intersection of physical chemistry methodology and
computational data practice, and *PCCP* has a strong record of publishing method-and-data work
where reproducibility and negative results are part of the argument.

**Reproducibility.** Code, pre-registrations and weekly evidence packages are released; the
submission ships no third-party dataset content, and third-party layers are reproducible by
path and SHA-256 (see the enclosed reproducibility statement).

**Declarations.** No competing interests. Data and code availability are stated in the
manuscript and the reproducibility statement.
